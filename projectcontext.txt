Cluby Documentation
Introduction
Problem Statement: Freshers often find it challenging to connect with clubs that match their interests due to limited social circles. As a result, they miss out on opportunities to showcase their skills and participate in campus events.
Solution Overview: "Cluby" aims to eliminate these barriers by creating a centralized platform where students can easily explore, apply for, and engage with clubs and college events.
Features of Cluby
Clublist: Displays active clubs on campus, providing details such as club objectives, events, and activities they organize.
Recruit: Lists available positions within college clubs and departments, enabling students to apply for roles within these organizations.
Community: A social space where clubs can post announcements, events, and activities, fostering engagement and interaction.
Workflow Description
Club Registration: Club Coordinators apply to list their clubs on Cluby. The Admin reviews the club details and, upon verification, approves or rejects the club.

Event & Vacancy Posting: Once approved, Club Representatives can post about upcoming events and vacancies in the Community and Recruit sections, making it visible to students.

Student Engagement: Students can view the list of clubs, follow or join clubs, and apply for posted vacancies. Club Coordinators have the authority to accept or reject student requests.

5. Club Profile Page
Purpose: A dedicated page for each club, showcasing its details, events, and recruitment information.
Key Elements:
Club description, mission, and members.
Recent posts about events or recruitment.
Option for students to request to join or follow the club.
Social media or external links related to the club.
6. Recruitment Page
Purpose: Display a list of available positions in clubs or departments that students can apply for.
Key Elements:
List of current vacancies.
Details about each post, such as job role, requirements, and application deadline.
"Apply" button for students to submit applications.
Filtering options (by clubs or departments).
Admin Dashboard
Purpose: Provide administrative control for managing clubs, monitoring activities, and addressing fraudulent behavior.
Key Elements:


Overview of all clubs and activities.
Tools for blacklisting users or clubs involved in fraudulent activities.
Data and analytics on platform usage.
7. Community Page
Purpose: A shared space for clubs to post general announcements, events, and engage with students.
Key Elements:
Posts from various clubs about events, activities, or announcements.
Comment sections for students to interact with posts.
Search bar and filters (e.g., by event type or date).
8. Student Dashboard
Purpose: A personal dashboard for students to manage club applications, events, and interactions.
Key Elements:
List of joined clubs.
Status of club membership requests (pending/approved/rejected).
Upcoming events from joined or followed clubs.
Notifications about new events, posts, or recruitment updates.

9. Club Representative Dashboard
Purpose: Provide tools for club representatives to manage club posts, events, and recruitments.
Key Elements:
Create new posts for events or recruitment.
Manage club profile and edit details.
View and approve/reject student requests to join.

2. Platform Overview
Key Features
Club Registration & Management: Allows clubs to register, manage profiles, and post vacancies or events.
Event Management: A centralized system to view and RSVP to college events.
Recruitment Opportunities: A page dedicated to internships, job openings, and other recruitment opportunities within clubs.
Community Page: A space for clubs to post announcements, discussions, and updates.
User Dashboards: Custom dashboards for students, club representatives, and admins to track activity and manage tasks.
Notifications & Alerts: Real-time updates about club activities, event reminders, and recruitment deadlines.

Core Modules
User Authentication & Roles: Allows users to sign in with role-based access (Students, CRs, Admins).
Club Management System: Provides tools for managing club profiles and posts.
Event Calendar & RSVP: Allows students to register for events and track deadlines.
Recruitment Management: A page dedicated to showcasing available club positions
Community Posts: Enables clubs to share announcements and news with students.


3. User Roles and Permissions
Admin
Permissions:
Approve or reject club registrations.
Modify club details and deactivate fraudulent clubs.
Monitor student activity and manage reported content.
Oversee recruitment opportunities.
Approve or reject community posts and events.
Responsibilities: Manage platform, monitor activities, and ensure the smooth operation of the platform.
Club Representative (CR)
Permissions:
Create, edit, and manage club profiles.
Post and manage vacancies, events, and recruitment opportunities.
Engage with students through community posts.
Responsibilities: Represent a club on the platform, manage club activities, and recruit members.
Student
Permissions:
Explore and join clubs.
RSVP to events and apply for recruitment opportunities.
Participate in community discussions and like/comment on posts.
Responsibilities: Engage with the platform, join clubs, participate in events, and apply for roles.

4. Feature Breakdown
Club Registration & Management
For Admin: Admin can create a club account by filling out details provided by the club representative and approve new clubs.
For Club Representatives: Once logged in, CRs can fill out a form to complete their club profile (including name, bio, events, social media, and team members).
For Students: Students can view the list of clubs, explore their details, and follow them.
Event Management
For CRs: CRs can post events for their clubs, manage event details, and track RSVPs from students.
For Students: Students can view upcoming events, RSVP, and mark their attendance.
Recruitment Opportunities
For CRs: CRs can post vacancies for club positions or internships.
For Students: Students can view available vacancies, apply for positions, and track their applications.
Community Page
For CRs: CRs can post announcements, news, and engage with students via the community page.
For Students: Students can view posts, like and comment on them, and share on social media.
User Dashboards
For Admins: Admins can monitor user activity, approve/reject clubs, and track platform engagement.
For CRs: CRs can track member applications, post vacancies, and manage events.
For Students: Students can track their joined clubs, RSVP-ed events, and application statuses.
Notifications and Alerts
Automated reminders for students about event dates, recruitment deadlines, and club activities.

5. Workflow
Club Creation & Management
Admin receives a request for a new club registration.
Admin creates a club profile after receiving the details from the club representative.
Club representative logs in and completes the club profile.
Club Representative Workflow
CR logs in, manages club profiles, posts events, and recruitment opportunities.
CR interacts with students through the community page.
CR monitors event participation and manages applications.
Student Engagement
Students sign up and explore clubs, events, and recruitment opportunities.
Students RSVP for events and apply for roles.
Students interact with posts on the community page.
Admin Management
Admin monitors platform usage, approves clubs, and handles reported content.
Admin manages recruitment transparency and event approvals.
Admin sends global announcements and updates.

6. Admin Features

User Management
